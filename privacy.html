Privacy policy

A clear explanation of the information used when you visit this website and sign in to your account.

Last updated September 28, 2026

Information used

If you choose to sign in with Google, the site receives the profile information Google makes available for authentication, such as your name, email address, profile photo, and a user identifier. You can use the site without signing in unless a feature asks you to authenticate.

The site also includes a search field. Search text is submitted as a URL query, so it may appear in your browser history and in routine web-server logs.

How it is used

Account information is used to confirm your signed-in state, show your name and profile photo in the account menu, and provide sign-in and sign-out controls. Search text is used as the submitted query.

This site does not ask for or receive your Google password. Authentication is handled by Google and Firebase Authentication.

Google and Firebase

Signing in involves Google and Firebase Authentication, which process information under their own privacy terms. Their services may use cookies or similar technologies to maintain authentication and protect account security.

Review Google’s Privacy Policy and Firebase’s privacy information for details about their handling of data.

Your choices

Signing in is optional. You can sign out from the account menu, and you can manage third-party account permissions in your Google Account settings. You can also use your browser controls to clear cookies and site data; doing so may sign you out.

To ask about access to or deletion of information associated with your VishVeda account, contact the site operator using the contact details provided to you by VishVeda.

Retention and safety

Authentication data is handled by Firebase Authentication and Google as part of providing sign-in. The website displays profile details for the duration and purposes of the signed-in experience. No method of online transmission or storage can be guaranteed completely secure.

Policy updates

This policy may be updated when the website or its services change. The latest version and its update date will be published on this page.
